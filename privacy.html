Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026 · Applies to Slow Journey by Chingalo Family

Slow Journey (“the App”) is published by Chingalo Family. This policy explains what information the App handles and how we treat it. Slow Journey is designed to be offline-first. In Phase 1 you can use the full personal loop without creating a cloud account, without an internet connection, and without sending journal data to our servers.

2. Information the App stores on your device

Depending on how you use Slow Journey, the following may be stored on your device in a local SQLite database and related files (including photos in the app documents area):

  • Local profile: the name you enter, and optionally email and birthday. Theme preference (muted light or muted dark) is stored with the profile.
  • Optional PIN: a four-digit device lock, stored as a hash — not the digits themselves. Auto-lock timing (2, 5, or 10 minutes) is stored in local preferences if you turn it on. There is no cloud PIN reset in Phase 1.
  • Morning intentions: up to three aims per calendar day, including whether you marked them honored.
  • Evening reflections: what you learned, celebrations and wins, optional title, gratitude score, theme tags, and an optional photo you attach.
  • Growth counters: reflection totals, current and longest streaks, and per-tag learning counts derived from completed evenings.
  • Monthly insight: a narrative generated on the device from your completed days.
  • Reminder settings: whether Plan My Day and Reflect & Celebrate are on, and the clock times you choose.
  • Local diagnostics: app logs kept on the device for troubleshooting. They are not a sync candidate in Phase 1.

Email and birthday are optional. You can use Slow Journey with a name alone. Photos you add to a reflection stay on this device in Phase 1.

3. What we do not collect in Phase 1

For the shipped personal loop:

  • We do not require a cloud account, password, or internet connection.
  • We do not upload profile, journal, photos, PIN, or usage counters to Chingalo Family servers.
  • We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers in the App.
  • We do not send push notifications through a server. Reminders are scheduled by the operating system on this phone after you allow notifications.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Business rows in the local database include a syncStatus field so a future Phase 2 can add optional cloud sync without a breaking migration. That sync is not shipped. Until a future version clearly offers it and you choose to use it, data remains on the device.

4. Notifications

If you enable Plan My Day or Reflect & Celebrate, Slow Journey asks the operating system for notification permission and schedules local invitations (default 07:00 and 21:00). The wording is chosen on device from a local copy pool. These notifications are not delivered through Chingalo Family servers.

5. Export and wipe

You may export a JSON file of local data onto the device. Wipe local data permanently removes reflections, intentions, counters, and your profile from that installation. This cannot be undone. Uninstalling the App also removes on-device files unless the operating system retains an app backup you control.

6. This website

The Slow Journey marketing site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the site may result in standard web logs processed by GitHub (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) according to GitHub’s own policies. This website does not embed advertising or analytics scripts of our own.

If you email, call, or message us, we will use the details you send only to respond to your request.

7. Children

Slow Journey is a personal reflection tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a cloud service in Phase 1. Because data stays on the device, a parent or guardian who installs the App controls that installation.

8. Data security

We rely on the operating system’s app sandbox to keep the local database and photos private to Slow Journey. An optional PIN adds a lock screen when opening the App or after auto-lock. A PIN is not a substitute for device encryption or a stolen-device policy. Keep your phone locked.

9. Changes

If Slow Journey later offers cloud accounts, sync, or community features, we will update this policy before those features collect data off the device, and the App will describe the choice clearly. Continued use after an update posted here applies to that version.
