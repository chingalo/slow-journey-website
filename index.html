The daily cycle

Morning stillness. Evening rest.

Instead of maximizing task output, Slow Journey structures each day as a small loop: set intentions, live the day, then reflect and celebrate.

Morning · Set Intentions

Welcome the day with stillness. Focus your energy on up to three intentions to guide your journey, then tap Set My Day, or skip into the planner.

Evening · Reflect & Celebrate

Write what you learned, name your wins, choose themes and a gratitude face, optionally add a photo, then complete the day. Incomplete aims never punish your streak.

Inside the app

Four tabs. One unhurried loop.

After a cream splash and a short primer, Slow Journey lives in Feed, Planner, Growth, and Setup, a floating pill on phones, a sage rail on larger screens.

Journey Feed

A journal of past evenings: photos, titles, learnings, and theme chips. A sage streak badge keeps the current count close.

Daily Planner

Week or month calendars, a checklist for the day you are viewing, wisdom quotes, and a gentle path into reflection.

Growth

Reflections, current and longest streaks, weekly evening bars, ranked key-learning tags, and a monthly insight, computed on this device.

Setup

A local profile, muted dark theme, optional four-digit PIN with auto-lock, reminder times, and wipe. Your profile stays on this device, no cloud account.

Gentle reminders

Plan My Day and Reflect & Celebrate are invitations on this phone, never server push. Copy changes from day to day. Defaults are 07:00 and 21:00.

Muted Light & Dark

Sage on cream by day; forest canvas, cream titles, and light sage accents at night. Fraunces headings and Nunito body keep the voice warm.

A look inside

Captured from Slow Journey on iPhone, the same cream, sage, and sprout you meet at launch.

On this device

Airplane-mode safe by design.

Your journal never needs a login or a network. Name, optional email and birthday, intentions, reflections, photos, and PIN hash live in your phone. Reminders use the phone’s own notification schedule.

No account

Nothing is sent to a server to begin. Name is enough; email and birthday are optional.

PIN stays here

A four-digit lock hashes on device. There is no cloud reset.

Photos stay here

Evening pictures are cropped on the phone and stay there.

You can wipe

Wipe local data removes the profile and journal. It cannot be undone.
